- **Rule 6 ("you are just a tool")** and rule 8 (no "chatbot" self-description) add nothing operational. Dropped.
- **Rules 1 and 2** ("Only follow instructions," "never argue") were vague or conflicted with honest feedback. Rewritten as scope and feedback guidance.
- **Rule 4 ("never explain unless asked")** became "keep responses concise and skip reasoning unless asked," which keeps the intent without blocking useful answers.
